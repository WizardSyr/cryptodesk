How to Install in 1 Minute
1
Tap Download iPadOS_Cards.deb in Safari on your iPad. When prompted, choose Open in Filza.
2
In Filza, tap iPadOS_Cards.deb, then tap Install in the top-right corner. It will extract cleanly to /var/mobile/Library/Widgets/Homescreen/.
3
Go to Settings > Xen HTML > Homescreen > Add Widget. Choose iPadOS Clock, CryptoDesk Large Card, or iPadOS Weather Large Card and position them wherever you want!
