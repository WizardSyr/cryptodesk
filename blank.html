Invisible Spacer Icons

Install 6 completely invisible spacer icons to fill the top row on your homescreen, so your apps sit cleanly below your widgets!

📥 1-Tap Install Profile (6 Icons)
How It Works:
  1. Tap 1-Tap Install Profile above.
  2. Tap Install in the top-right corner of Settings.
  3. Go to your Homescreen: you will see 6 invisible spots.
  4. Hold any icon until they wiggle, then drag the 6 invisible icons to the top row (Row 1).
  5. Your real apps (Clock, FaceTime, Music, Photos) will naturally slide down to Row 2 and 3!

💡 To remove anytime: Go to Settings ➔ General ➔ Profiles ➔ Blank Grid Spacers ➔ Delete Profile.
