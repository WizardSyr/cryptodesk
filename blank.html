Invisible Grid Spacers

🛡️ 100% Native iOS • Zero-Crash Guarantee

Install 12 completely invisible, transparent spacer tiles to leave room for your widgets anywhere on your home screen!

📥 1-Tap Install Profile (12 Spacers)
How to Use Without Any Crash:
  1. Tap 1-Tap Install Profile above.
  2. When Settings opens, tap Install in the top-right corner (enter your passcode if asked, then tap Install again).
  3. Go to your Homescreen: you now have 12 invisible spacer tiles.
  4. Hold any icon until they jiggle, then drag the invisible tiles to Row 1 & Row 2 (behind where your widgets sit).
  5. Your real apps (Clock, FaceTime, Music, Photos) will cleanly drop down to lower rows!
  6. Press the Home button to save — it will NEVER crash or respring because it uses native iOS WebClips with zero Substrate hooks!
💡 How to delete: You can delete any unused invisible tile by tapping (X) in jiggle mode, or remove all of them together in Settings ➔ General ➔ Profile ➔ Blank Grid Spacers ➔ Delete Profile.
