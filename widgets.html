📲 How to Install as Homescreen Widgets (Xen HTML)
1
Download iPadOS_Cards.deb directly on your iPad in Safari, then tap Open in Filza.
2
In Filza, tap iPadOS_Cards.deb and press Install (at top right).
3
Go to Settings > Xen HTML > Homescreen > Add Widget, and select your new widgets:
• iPadOS Minimal Clock
• iPadOS Weather Card
• iPadOS Analog LA Clock
• iPadOS Spotify Player
